# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **hello@indianopinion.org** with "newsapi-pull security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version. We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days and aim to ship a fix, or a clear plan, within 14 days. We will credit you in the changelog unless you ask us not to.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release receives security fixes.

## Scope

In scope: the plugin's own code, including key handling, escaping, capability and nonce checks, and the de-duplication table. Out of scope: NewsAPI.org itself, and WordPress core.
